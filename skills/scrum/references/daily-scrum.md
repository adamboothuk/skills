# Daily Scrum

Use this reference when the user wants to improve the Daily Scrum or diagnose why it has become a status meeting.

## Purpose

The Daily Scrum is for Developers to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the Sprint Backlog.

## Healthy Signals

- Developers talk to each other, not only to a manager or Scrum Master.
- The Sprint Goal is visible in the conversation.
- The team adapts the plan when new information appears.
- Follow-up problem solving happens after the 15-minute event.

## Improvement Workflow

1. Ask what decision or adaptation the Daily Scrum currently enables.
2. Re-anchor the conversation on the Sprint Goal.
3. Make current work, blockers, and flow visible.
4. Move detailed problem solving outside the event.
5. Experiment with a structure for one Sprint, then inspect.

## Useful Prompts

- Are we still on track for the Sprint Goal?
- What changed since yesterday that affects our plan?
- Where is work stuck?
- What needs collaboration today?
- What should we discuss immediately after this event?

## Failure Modes

- Reporting individual activity instead of adapting the plan.
- Letting non-Developers turn it into a supervision meeting.
- Solving every issue inside the timebox.
- Ignoring the Sprint Goal.
