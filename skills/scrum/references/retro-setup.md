# Retro Setup

Use this reference when the user wants to design or prepare a Sprint Retrospective.

## Purpose

Help the Scrum Team inspect how the last Sprint went with regard to individuals, interactions, processes, tools, and Definition of Done, then decide useful improvements.

## Inputs

- Sprint Goal and whether it was met.
- Any notable delivery, quality, collaboration, or stakeholder events.
- Existing improvement actions from previous retrospectives.
- Team mood, psychological safety, and available time.

## Setup Workflow

1. Clarify the retrospective objective: learning, repair, process improvement, team health, or decision-making.
2. Choose a format that fits the objective and team safety level.
3. Prepare a focused prompt, not a broad complaint board.
4. Timebox divergent thinking, grouping, discussion, and action selection.
5. Plan how the team will choose one or two realistic improvements.
6. Decide how actions will be visible during the next Sprint.

## Format Options

- Simple inspect/adapt: What helped? What got in the way? What will we try next?
- Timeline: useful after a complex or emotional Sprint.
- Start/stop/continue: useful for quick operational improvement.
- Lean coffee: useful when the team has many topics and ownership is high.
- 4Ls: liked, learned, lacked, longed for; useful for broader reflection.

## Output

A good retrospective setup should produce:

- A clear purpose for the session.
- A facilitation plan with timings.
- One or two candidate improvement actions.
- A lightweight follow-up mechanism for the next Sprint.

## Common Failure Modes

- Trying to solve too many issues.
- Turning the event into delivery status reporting.
- Selecting actions no one owns.
- Creating actions outside the team's influence.
- Ignoring previous retrospective actions.
