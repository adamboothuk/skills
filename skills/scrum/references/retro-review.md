# Retro Review

Use this reference when the user wants to review retrospective outcomes, assess action quality, or turn retro notes into improvement work.

## Review Questions

- Did the team identify a real problem, or only a symptom?
- Is the action inside the Scrum Team's influence?
- Is the action small enough to try in one Sprint?
- Is the expected benefit clear?
- Is there a moment planned to inspect whether it worked?

## Action Quality Checklist

A strong retrospective action is:

- Specific enough that the team knows what will change.
- Small enough to attempt during the next Sprint.
- Owned by the team, not delegated vaguely to someone else.
- Connected to a problem the team actually discussed.
- Visible in the next Sprint's work or working agreements.

## Turning Notes Into Actions

1. Group related observations.
2. Identify the underlying theme.
3. Separate team-owned improvements from wider organizational impediments.
4. Convert one or two team-owned improvements into experiments.
5. Capture wider impediments transparently, with the next escalation or conversation.

## Example Action Shapes

- For one Sprint, we will add acceptance examples before starting each Product Backlog item.
- For one Sprint, we will reserve 15 minutes after Daily Scrum twice a week for dependency follow-up.
- For one Sprint, we will update the Definition of Done checklist before moving work to done.

## Common Failure Modes

- Treating every retro note as an action.
- Creating actions that are really management requests.
- Losing the action before the next Sprint starts.
- Never checking whether previous actions helped.
