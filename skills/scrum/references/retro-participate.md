# Retro Participation

Use this reference when the user wants help facilitating, participating in, or adapting a live Sprint Retrospective.

## Facilitation Stance

- Keep the focus on learning and improvement, not blame.
- Invite quieter voices without forcing personal disclosure.
- Separate facts, interpretations, feelings, and proposed actions.
- Watch for topics that need a decision outside the retrospective.

## In-Session Workflow

1. Restate the purpose and timebox.
2. Check whether previous improvement actions changed anything.
3. Gather observations silently before group discussion where possible.
4. Group related topics.
5. Help the team choose the most valuable topic to inspect.
6. Move from symptoms to causes before action selection.
7. End with a concrete experiment or improvement action.

## Participation Prompts

- What made progress easier this Sprint?
- Where did we lose time or clarity?
- What surprised us?
- What should we try for one Sprint, then inspect?
- What is inside our control?

## When Discussion Gets Stuck

- If it becomes blame-focused, ask what system condition made the problem easier to repeat.
- If it becomes too abstract, ask for a recent concrete example.
- If it becomes solution-jumping, ask what problem the solution is meant to solve.
- If energy drops, narrow to one small experiment.

## Output

A useful session ends with a visible action or experiment, an owner or follow-up point, and an agreement on when the team will inspect whether it helped.
