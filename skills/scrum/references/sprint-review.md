# Sprint Review

Use this reference when the user wants help preparing or improving a Sprint Review.

## Purpose

The Sprint Review inspects the outcome of the Sprint and determines future adaptations. It is a working session with stakeholders, not just a demo.

## Inputs

- Sprint Goal and outcome.
- Current Increment.
- Product Backlog and Product Goal.
- Market, stakeholder, user, or business changes.
- Feedback and usage evidence where available.

## Workflow

1. Reconnect everyone to the Product Goal and Sprint Goal.
2. Show the current Increment and explain what changed.
3. Discuss what was learned, including work not done if relevant.
4. Invite stakeholder feedback on product direction and value.
5. Discuss changes that may affect Product Backlog ordering.
6. Capture follow-up decisions, questions, and Product Backlog updates.

## Good Review Questions

- What does this Increment make possible now?
- What did we learn that should affect the Product Backlog?
- Has anything changed in the market, organization, or user need?
- What feedback should shape the next Sprint Planning conversation?

## Failure Modes

- Running only a scripted demo.
- Avoiding discussion of missed Sprint Goal or incomplete work.
- Collecting feedback without updating Product Backlog decisions.
- Inviting stakeholders too late to influence direction.
